Facilities and family travel

LTC (central government): one hometown and one anywhere-in-India journey per 4-year block (2026–2029) for you and eligible family, after one year of service. Railways: privilege passes. Public sector banks: usually Leave Fare Concession every 2 years. PSUs and ports: staff guest houses and their own leave-travel schemes.

Circuit houses, inspection bungalows, forest and railway rest houses are mainly for official tours and are allotted by grade and availability. Family stay is usually at your own cost or with permission, so confirm the rule for each department.

Best fit for you: Mumbai Port, AIIMS Rishikesh, Oil India, NTPC, RITES, and Bank of India Scale III/IV if you have the experience.

Documents to keep ready (scan each as PDF/JPG)

Identity and age
10th marksheet or certificate showing date of birth (17-Oct-1990), Aadhaar or other photo ID.
Education
B.Tech degree or provisional certificate, all semester marksheets, and a percentage/aggregate certificate. Your 59.42% must be shown clearly if the post needs a minimum.
Experience (needed for bank and senior posts)
Experience certificates on letterhead with joining date, post and duties; last salary slips; relieving letters. Certifications such as CISA/CISM/CISSP if the post asks.
Photo and signature
Recent passport photo, signature in black ink. Bank of India also needs a left thumb impression, a handwritten declaration, and a live photo taken by webcam or phone during the form.
Category and special certificates (if you claim them)
OBC non-creamy layer certificate issued on or after 1 Apr 2026; EWS income and asset certificate for FY 2025-26; SC/ST certificate; a No Objection Certificate if you work in a government department or PSU.
Other
Working email and mobile number, and net banking or card for the fee (Bank of India: ₹1,180 for General/OBC/EWS). Match every name and date exactly to your certificates.
